How does measuring engagement change Netflix viewing patterns?

Netflix viewing data, January–June 2025

Average Hours Viewed by Runtime

Average Views by Runtime

Findings

Average hours viewed increase with runtime, while average views decrease. The choice of metric changes how we interpret Netflix engagement.

Methodology

Netflix calculates views by dividing hours viewed by runtime. Because the metrics are mathematically related, these results do not establish viewer preferences.

Source: Netflix, What We Watched (January–June 2025). Netflix's viewing methodology .
